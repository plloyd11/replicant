# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Existing Replicant fans, mostly arriving from Instagram, Bandcamp, a show, or a press premiere. They already know the band and come to the site to buy merch: records, shirts, patches, limited drops. Many are on a phone. New listeners come too, but they are secondary; the site serves the fan who is ready to buy.

## Product Purpose

replicant.band is the official home and merch store for Replicant, a dissonant technical death metal band from New Jersey. Snipcart handles checkout directly on the site. Success means a fan lands on the site, sees what's new, and buys it in as few steps as possible. Music (streaming players, discography, press) gives the merch its context and backs up the band's credibility.

## Brand Personality

Dissonant, mechanical, cosmic, with a straight face that cracks into New Jersey humor.

- **Dissonant & mechanical:** cold, precise, machine-built. The music is technical, and the site should feel engineered, not thrown together.
- **Cosmic & sci-fi:** deep-space dread, Blade Runner lineage (the band name), transmissions from somewhere far away.
- **Irreverent NJ humor:** "Respect New Jersey or prepare for combat." "Drums, Cigars." The band is brutal but not self-serious. Humor lives in the microcopy and never undercuts the music.

The site should make a fan feel they are dealing with the band directly, not with a label or a storefront.

## Anti-references

- **Generic metal template:** blackletter logos, flames, skulls, gore for shock value, cheap grunge brushes.
- **Shopify store hero:** product carousels, SALE banners, generic e-commerce slickness.
- **SaaS polish:** soft gradients, glassmorphism, rounded cards, startup-landing vibes.
- **Label promo graphic:** busy flyer-style compositions crammed with logos, dates, badges, and stickers.

## Design Principles

1. **Store first, stage second.** Every surface should make buying easy for a fan who already knows the band. Music and press frame the merch; they don't bury it.
2. **Let the artwork do the shouting.** Album and merch art are the strongest visual assets. Design frames them and builds from their palette and texture rather than competing with them.
3. **Machine precision over metal cliché.** The identity is cold, technical, and cosmic, carried by structure, type, and restraint, not by genre signifiers.
4. **Brutal, not self-serious.** Copy can wink. Layout and craft never do.
5. **One maintainer.** A single person updates the site and CMS. Favor designs that are easy to swap, update, or retire (e.g. a release hero that can be replaced with one line).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large display type and UI boundaries. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all entrance and ambient animation. Interactive elements are keyboard reachable with visible focus states. Album art always carries descriptive alt text.
